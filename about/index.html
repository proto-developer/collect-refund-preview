Why CollectRefund exists

Billions in unclaimed property. A process built for none of it.

Billions of dollars in unclaimed property are held by state governments, but recovering it is often confusing, slow, and difficult to navigate. CollectRefund was built to make the process easier by helping people find unclaimed money, verify ownership, and understand exactly what is needed to recover funds that already belong to them.

No upfront cost to start

There is no upfront cost to search or begin the process, any recovery fee is disclosed before recovery is authorized.

Clear, guided recovery process

We help determine what is needed and guide you through each step.

Real support from real people

Our U.S.-based team combines smart technology with hands-on help when it matters most.

Founder story

Built from a personal mission

CollectRefund is led by David Dorfman, Co-Founder & CEO, and was inspired by the experience of a close friend who saw firsthand how frustrating and outdated the recovery process can be. Our mission is to bring more trust, clarity, and hands-on support to families reclaiming what is rightfully theirs.

How we work

Technology plus human support

Software does the searching and watching. People handle the judgment calls.

CollectRefund’s technology searches available state records, matches them against names, addresses, and entity histories, and monitors 250M+ records nightly so new funds don’t go unnoticed.

When a claim needs documentation, state-specific forms, or a judgment call, a real person steps in, reviewing paperwork, coordinating notarization where required, and following the claim through to payment.
