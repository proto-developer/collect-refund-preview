  • Identification of unclaimed property held by government agencies

  • Communication with state and federal agencies on your behalf

As a client, you agree to:

4. Fees and Payment

Our fee structure is contingency-based:

  • We charge a percentage of the recovered amount as specified in your service agreement
  • No upfront fees are required
  • You only pay if we successfully recover funds on your behalf
