Privacy boundary.

What employers see, and what they never see.

Employers may receive aggregate enrollment, engagement, match, and recovery totals. CollectRefund does not share individual employee search results, claim details, addresses, or personal recovery amounts with employers.

CollectRefund encrypts personal information in transit and at rest. Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to provide services, maintain required records, and meet legal obligations. Users may request deletion by emailing info@collectrefund.com.

CollectRefund for Work questions, answered

What does CollectRefund for Work cost?

Managed recovery in are free during launch. If an employee chooses CollectRefund Recovery, a preferred recovery rate may apply based on the employer agreement. Any fee is disclosed before recovery is authorized.

What can our company see about employee activity?

Employers may receive aggregate enrollment, engagement, match, and recovery totals. CollectRefund does not share individual employee search results, claim details, addresses, or personal recovery amounts with employers.

How much administration does the benefit require?

Very little. CollectRefund provides a dedicated enrollment link, ready-made campaign materials, and handles enrollment, identity confirmation, monitoring, and recovery directly with each employee. There is no payroll integration or ongoing administration required.

Who is CollectRefund for Work designed for?

Employers with 100 or more employees, benefits brokers, PEOs, and payroll providers, typically championed by total rewards, HR, benefits, and people-operations leaders.
