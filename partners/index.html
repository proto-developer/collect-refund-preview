Partnership questions, answered

How does CollectRefund work as an employee benefit?

CollectRefund for Work gives your employees access to unclaimed-property Managed recovery in. Managed recovery in are free during launch. If an employee chooses CollectRefund Recovery, a preferred recovery rate may apply based on the employer agreement. Any fee is disclosed before recovery is authorized.

What do employers see about employee activity?

Employers may receive aggregate enrollment, engagement, match, and recovery totals. CollectRefund does not share individual employee search results, claim details, addresses, or personal recovery amounts with employers.

How is partnership pricing structured?

Partnership pricing, recovery fees, and any commercial or revenue-sharing terms are established in the applicable partner agreement.
