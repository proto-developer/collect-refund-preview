How Unclaimed Property Works in Georgia

Unlike most states, Georgia’s unclaimed property program is run by the Georgia Department of Revenue (DOR), not the State Treasurer’s office. The program operates under the Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act (O.C.G.A. § 44-12-190 et seq.).

When a financial account, paycheck, refund, or insurance benefit has had no owner-initiated activity for the dormancy period, generally five years, the holder is legally required to escheat the property to the state. Georgia then holds it in trust indefinitely until the rightful owner or heir files a valid claim.

What Types of Property Are Held by Georgia

  • Bank accounts and uncashed checks
  • Insurance proceeds
  • Stocks and dividends (from Georgia-based and Georgia-registered corporations)
  • Utility and rental deposits
  • Unclaimed wages (1-year dormancy, the shortest category)
  • Refunds (vendor, tax, insurance overpayments)
  • Safe deposit box contents

How to Search the Georgia Unclaimed Property Database

The official Georgia search portal is gaclaims.unclaimedproperty.com, accessible from the Department of Revenue website. Search tips:

  • every name variation
  • every Georgia address (including college and military addresses)
  • former Georgia employers as holder name (major Atlanta-headquartered companies generate the largest single source of unclaimed wages and stock)
  • relatives who lived or worked in Georgia
  • don’t rely solely on MissingMoney.com — the state’s own database is authoritative

How the Georgia Claim Process Works

The Georgia DOR has modernized its claim process substantially in recent years. Many low-dollar cash claims can be initiated online, while larger or more complex claims follow a documentation-heavy path.

A typical Georgia claim packet requires:

  • completed claim form (generated by the Georgia portal)
  • government-issued photo ID
  • proof of association with the listed property
  • for inherited property: death certificate, letters testamentary, will, proof of heirship
  • for business claims: corporate records, EIN, signing authority
  • for securities: documentation of original ownership and corporate action history

Simple cash claims often resolve in 60–120 days. Securities, estate, and business claims commonly run 6–12 months and may require multiple rounds of supplemental documentation.

When to Consider Professional Help

  • is multi-state (common for executives or contractors who worked for Atlanta-based companies while living elsewhere)

What Makes Georgia Different

  • Department of Revenue, not Treasury. Georgia is unusual in housing unclaimed property under the DOR rather than the Treasurer’s office. Practically, this means correspondence and forms come from the DOR.
  • 5-year dormancy. Property reaches Georgia more slowly than states like California or New York, which use 3-year dormancy. This means Georgia accumulates somewhat more recent property.
  • Wages have an unusually short dormancy. At just 1 year, unclaimed wages reach the state quickly. If you left a Georgia employer recently, your final paycheck or unused PTO payout may already be listed.
  • Atlanta corporate concentration. A disproportionate volume of Georgia’s unclaimed property originates from large Atlanta-headquartered employers: Coca-Cola, Delta Air Lines, UPS, Home Depot, NCR, and others.

What to Know About Georgia’s Unclaimed Property Program

  1. Georgia’s unclaimed property program is administered by the Department of Revenue, not the State Treasurer’s office, as in many other states.
  2. Georgia uses a 5-year dormancy period for most property, meaning property reaches the state more slowly than in states like California (3 years).
  3. Atlanta’s position as a major corporate headquarters hub (Coca-Cola, Delta, UPS, Home Depot) means a high volume of payroll, vendor, and securities claims flow through Georgia.
  4. Georgia returns tens of millions of dollars per year to rightful owners, but the unclaimed property fund continues to grow each reporting cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions: Georgia Unclaimed Property

How much unclaimed property does Georgia hold?

The Georgia Department of Revenue holds over $1.5 billion in unclaimed property, with millions of individual properties listed in the state database.

Where do I search Georgia unclaimed property?

The official Georgia search portal is gaclaims.unclaimedproperty.com, accessible from the Department of Revenue website at dor.georgia.gov.

Why is Georgia’s unclaimed property handled by the Department of Revenue?

Georgia is one of a small number of states that houses its unclaimed property program under the Department of Revenue rather than the State Treasurer’s office. This is a structural choice and does not affect your right to claim, but it does mean correspondence comes from the DOR.

How long does a Georgia unclaimed property claim take?

Simple cash claims often resolve in 60 to 120 days. Securities, estate, and business claims commonly take 6 to 12 months and may require additional documentation.

What is Georgia’s dormancy period?

Most property types in Georgia have a 5-year dormancy period before escheatment. Unclaimed wages have a much shorter 1-year dormancy.

Is there a deadline to claim unclaimed property in Georgia?

No. Georgia holds unclaimed property indefinitely. There is no deadline to claim and no statute of limitations on filing.
